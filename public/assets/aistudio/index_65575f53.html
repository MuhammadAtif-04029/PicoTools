About

About ToolNest

One small toolbox for everyday file jobs

ToolNest brings the tools people reach for every day into one place: merge or split a PDF, shrink a photo, turn a video you own into an MP3. Instead of hunting through a dozen different sites, you get five clear categories, every tool in the same calm smoked-glass interface, and no accounts or sign-ups.

It is built to be fast and honest about what it does. The PDF and image tools run entirely inside your browser, so your files stay on your device. The video tools need a server to fetch and convert media, and we say so on the page.

Good to know

Private by design. PDF and image tools never upload your files.

Short-lived downloads. Converted media is deleted from the server after 30 minutes.

Use it responsibly. Only download content you own or have permission to use; we ask you to confirm every time.

Honest limits. Scanned PDFs need OCR, which is not supported. Remove Background works best on plain backgrounds. PDF ↔ Word keeps text only.

Anonymous counts. We count how often each tool is used, and nothing about you.
